How to add your group calendar

Copy the calendar link, then follow the steps for your app. The calendar updates whenever the schedule changes — you only need to add it once.

G
Google Calendar — computer or Android
  1. Open Google Calendar in your browser.
  2. On the left side, find Other calendars and click the + sign.
  3. Choose From URL.
  4. Paste the link you copied and click Add calendar.

Updates can take a few hours to appear.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Calendar, then Accounts.
  3. Tap Add Account, then Other.
  4. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar.
  5. Paste the link and tap Next, then Save.

The calendar appears in your iPhone Calendar app automatically.

Mac — Calendar app
  1. Open the Calendar app on your Mac.
  2. In the menu bar at the top, click File.
  3. Click New Calendar Subscription…
  4. Paste the link and click Subscribe.
  5. Choose how often to refresh, then click OK.
O
Outlook — browser or desktop app
  1. Open Outlook Calendar.
  2. Click Add calendar (top left or in the sidebar).
  3. Choose Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the link and click Import.

In the Outlook desktop app, go to File > Account Settings > Internet Calendars > New, then paste the link.
