ROW Meet Calendars 2026-27

Add your group's meets to your digital calendar. You get the meet dates and every Confirm By date.

To confirm or decline a meet, log into your ROW member account.

Subscribe, do not download. Copy your group's link and add it as a calendar by URL. If you download the file instead, you get a copy that never updates, and a moved meet will not move in your calendar.

How to subscribe

Google Calendar (do this on a computer): Other calendars, +, From URL, paste the link, Add calendar. It then appears on every device signed in to that account.

iPhone or iPad: Settings, Apps, Calendar, Calendar Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link.

Outlook: Add calendar, Subscribe from web, paste the link.
